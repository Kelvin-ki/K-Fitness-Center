# Food / Training video brief

Create a vertical 9:16, approximately 15-second fitness motivation reel. Use cinematic but natural photorealistic footage, clean black and gold typography, quick controlled cuts, and energetic instrumental music. Do not use a fabricated gym logo.

Sequence:
- 0–3s: Adult athlete finishes a controlled dumbbell set. Hook: “TRAINING HARD?”
- 3–8s: Attractive real meal preparation: chicken or tofu, eggs, rice, vegetables, and water. Headline: “FOOD 70%”.
- 8–11s: Controlled resistance training with a stable body and realistic weight. Headline: “TRAINING 30%”.
- 11–15s: Meal and training visuals together. Closing: “EAT WELL. TRAIN CONSISTENTLY. RECOVER WELL.”

Show a readable qualifier with the percentages: “Motivational idea • not a fixed scientific ratio”.
Avoid guaranteed outcomes, dramatic body transformations, anatomy distortion, excessive text, medical claims, and invented brand marks.
